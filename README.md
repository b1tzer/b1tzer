## 🧠 Currently Building

### Cognia

> An AI learning coach that understands what you know, what you don't know, and why.

Cognia is an experiment in building an AI that doesn't just answer questions, but actually **understands the learner**.

The idea is simple:

```text
Goal
 ↓
Knowledge Model
 ↓
Probe
 ↓
Diagnose
 ↓
Intervene
 ↓
Re-test
 ↓
Update Knowledge
 ↓
Repeat
```

The system tries to model concepts, dependencies, misconceptions and learning progress instead of treating every conversation as an isolated chat.

**Stack**

`Python` `LangGraph` `LangChain` `FastAPI` `PostgreSQL` `DeepSeek` `CopilotKit` `AG-UI`
